Receipt errors

What happens when a panelist declares that they received a package and something does not add up, what ONEMS fixes on its own, and what you decide on the Incidents › Receipts screen.

Overview

No failure gets lost

Every receipt with a problem opens a case: who declared it, which tag they typed, the reason and how it ended.

ONEMS fixes what is safe

A mistyped digit, or a receipt at another point in the same city, is corrected automatically, using the date of the first attempt.

What is not valid is compensated

If the receipt cannot be used for the measurement, the shipment is cancelled and goes to compensation: balancing schedules it again.

You decide the rest

Doubtful cases wait in Pending decisions, with the Fix and Compensate / Discard buttons.

How it works

1 · TelegramThe panelist declaresThey send the photo or the number of the tag of the package they received.
→
2 · ONEMSLooks for the shipmentIf everything adds up, the receipt is registered and no case is opened.
→
3 · ONEMSClassifies the problemIt fixes it, compensates it, records it as information or leaves the case pending.
→
4 · ONEMSThe manager decidesOnly in the cases the system cannot resolve safely.
  • The ONE bot answers the panelist with the real reason and what to do, in their language.
  • If the panelist repeats the same tag several times, it is a single case; the Attempts column counts the times.
  • Every receipt, including fixed ones, goes through the PO Box rule.

Where to find the screen

In the ONEMS menu, open the Incidents group and click Receipts. It is visible to the admin users of each account (and superadmin), and each account sees only its own panelists.

  1. In Pending decisions, click Fix on the case.
  2. ONEMS lists the sent shipments headed to the panelist’s city that have not yet been received, with their tag, the origin panelist and the date. If there are none, it says so: There are no sent shipments addressed to this panelist’s city.
  3. If in doubt, ask the panelist who sent them the package: panelists recognise the sender, not the product.
  4. Select the correct shipment and click Fix with this shipment.
What happens next: the case becomes Fixed, the receipt keeps the date of the first attempt, and ONEMS records who fixed it and when. The PO Box rule still applies: if it is not met, the case ends as Compensated.
Cases Telegram user not registered as a panelist and No node have no Fix button: without a panelist or without a point there is no city to search in. They can only be closed.

Compensate or discard

Use it when the receipt cannot be accepted as valid.

  1. Click Compensate / Discard on the case.
  2. Read the notice: if the case has a linked shipment that has not yet been received, that shipment will be cancelled and compensated; if it has no shipment, the case is simply discarded.
  3. Write a note if you want to record the reason (optional) and click Close case.
What happens next: the case becomes Compensated or Discarded, leaves Pending decisions and the menu badge goes down. A compensated shipment is scheduled again in the next balancing. This cannot be undone from the screen.

Automatic reminders

The panelist who caused a failure receives a Telegram message, in their language, explaining what happened and what to do next time. You do not have to do anything.

  • At most one reminder a day per panelist and reason.
  • Only between 08:00 and 20:00, local time of the account. Outside those hours it waits until 08:00.
  • From the third failure in 7 days, the message says their manager has been informed.
  • It is not sent if the panelist had declared unavailability, nor in cases with no responsible party (for example, Label of a parcel for another city).

Notice to a panelist

  1. In Repeat panelists, click Send notice on the panelist’s row.
  2. Notice to panelist opens with a text already prepared for their main reason, in their language. You can change the Language and edit the text.
  3. Click Send.
What happens next: the message reaches the panelist’s Telegram and the Last notice column shows the date.

Message to all panelists

For a mistake that many people make, this is better than a notice to each one.

  1. Click Message to all panelists in the top bar.
  2. Choose a Template: Lesson: visit your PO Box every day, Receipt cancelled: no visit the previous day, Sender: shipment not declared in the bot, Sender: declared tag did not match the envelope or Sender: letter arrived in another city. Edit the text if needed.
  3. Check the Recipients: the account’s active panelists with Telegram.
  4. Click Confirm: send to N panelists.
What happens next: each panelist receives it in their language. Under Previous messages you will find the date, the text, and how many were sent and how many failed.

Good to know

  • The answer depends on the reason: tag not found, already received, shipment not declared by the sender, PO Box not visited, and so on.
  • If ONEMS fixes a typo, the panelist sees their receipt confirmed as normal.
  • If the PO Box rule cancels the receipt, the bot does not say “received”.

Deadlines and rules

RuleWhat ONEMS does
Tag not foundIt searches again every 30 minutes for 5 working days, including the typo search. Only shipments declared before the first attempt count. If it turns up, the case is fixed on its own; if not, it moves to Needs decision.
TypoIt is corrected only if there is a single shipment sent to the panelist’s city whose tag differs by one digit or by two adjacent digits swapped. With several, you decide.
PO BoxA PO Box letter is valid only if the panelist declared some shipment on the previous working day (a sign that they visited the PO Box). If not, it is cancelled and compensated.
Package not receivedIf a shipment is not received within 15 days of its departure, it is cancelled and you receive an email. If it arrives later and the panelist declares it, the shipment is reactivated and the receipt is valid with the date of the attempt.
Another cityA shipment is never reassigned to another city: it would change the route being measured. That is why you decide this case.

Tag with one digit changed

The sender stuck on tag 00010884 but declared 00010894 in the bot.

Tag that does not exist

A panelist types 00010813 twice. There is no shipment with that tag, and none similar.

  1. The case is Pending and the bot asks her for a photo of the tag. In Reported incidents an Unreadable label / receipt incident appears with the photo.
  2. The photo reads 00010815, sent by Manasseh Ouma. In Receipts, click Fix, choose 00010815 and click Fix with this shipment.
  3. If it is impossible to tell which one it is, wait: if the sender declares it within the next 5 working days, it is fixed on its own.
What happens next: also resolve the incident in Reported incidents with a note.

Sender who did not declare the shipment

The package arrives, the tag is correct, but the sender never marked it as sent.

  • Without a departure date the time cannot be measured. The shipment is cancelled and goes to compensation: Not sent yet, cancelled, Compensated, with the sender responsible.
  • You: nothing. If it happens with several senders, use the template Sender: shipment not declared in the bot in the message to everyone.

PO Box not visited

A panelist with a PO Box declares a letter on Monday, but on the previous Friday they declared no shipment.

  • It is not known which day the letter arrived, so it is not valid: PO Box not visited, Compensated, with the receiver responsible.
  • If they were declared unavailable that Friday, the reason is PO Box not visited (declared unavailable) and it is not attributed to them.
  • You: nothing; if many people make the mistake, use the template Lesson: visit your PO Box every day.

Tag from another city

A panelist in Nairobi declares a tag that belongs to a shipment to Mombasa.

  1. The case is Needs decision and the Mombasa shipment remains intact.
  2. Ask the panelist about the sender or ask for a photo. If it was a typo, click Fix and choose their real shipment.
  3. If the package really reached the wrong city, click Compensate / Discard and leave a note.

Questions

Why don’t I see the red badge on Receipts?
Because there are no open cases in the account. It only appears when some case is Pending or Needs decision.
Why doesn’t the “Pending decisions” list change when I choose another date?
Because it shows the open cases from any day. The date only changes the summary and the Cases list.
If I fix a case days later, is the time measured wrongly?
No. The receipt is registered with the time of the panelist’s first attempt.
What happens to a compensated shipment?
It is cancelled and stays on the list of samples to compensate. Balancing schedules it again later.
Can I undo a closure?
Not from the screen. Contact support.
Does the 09:00 daily summary include these cases?
No. Receipt follow-up is only on this screen.
Who sees these cases?
The account’s admin users and superadmin. Each account sees only its own panelists.
