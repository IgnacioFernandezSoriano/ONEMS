Incidents and panelist availability

How panelists report problems and unavailable days through Telegram, what ONE resolves on its own, and what you decide as a manager.

Overview

Panelists use Telegram

They chat with the ONE bot in their own language (English, Spanish, French or Arabic) to confirm shipments, report a problem or declare unavailable days.

ONE resolves what it can

Many reports are closed automatically. Unavailable days are re-planned by moving samples to a later date or to another node in the same city.

Managers decide the rest

What cannot be resolved automatically appears in Incidents with a proposal ready, and every message is saved in the incident.

This page has two audiences. Panelist and bot sections describe what the panelist sees. Manager sections describe what you see in ONE.

How it works

→

  • Nothing is saved until the panelist answers Yes. If they answer No, the bot shows the menu again.
  • Everything the bot says, everything the panelist answers and everything you write is stored in the incident.
  • You get an email when a new incident needs you, and when a panelist reply has been waiting for more than an hour.

Starting the bot

Each panelist is linked to the ONE bot by their Telegram account. Open the chat and send /start, or just say hello. The bot greets them by first name and shows the main menu.

ONE bot
/start
Hi Marta, what do you need?
View pending shipmentsView packages to receiveConfirm a shipmentConfirm a receptionReport incident🗓️ Unavailable days📦 Confirm material received

Good to know

  • The bot answers in the language set in the panelist’s profile.
  • Typing something that is not a request (“Hello”, “thanks”) just shows the menu again.
  • /myid shows the panelist’s Telegram ID. Support may ask for it when linking an account.

Sending and receiving

Confirming a shipment

  1. The panelist taps Confirm a shipment or View pending shipments and picks the shipment.
  2. They send a photo of the label or type the label number.
  3. The bot replies that the shipment is declared as sent. If the label format is not valid, nothing is saved and the bot asks again.

Confirming a reception

  1. The panelist taps Confirm a reception.
  2. They send a photo of the label of the package received.
  3. ONE marks that sample as received. If the label is unreadable, they report it as an incident instead (Unreadable label / receipt), choose the package from their list and it is registered automatically.
Daily reminder. Every day at 09:00 the bot sends each panelist the shipments to send that day. Shipments that cannot be made because of missing material are left out of the reminder.

Reporting an incident

The panelist taps Report incident and describes the problem in a single message. The bot suggests a type and asks for confirmation.

A damaged parcel is saved and closed automatically.

ONE bot

A returned parcel stays open for the manager.

Safe by design. If the panelist taps No, or types something unclear, nothing is registered. Only an explicit Yes creates an incident.

Unavailable days

The panelist taps 🗓️ Unavailable days and chooses the start and end dates. They can also write it in plain words.

ONE bot
Next week I’m on holiday
I understood Mon 9 Nov – Fri 13 Nov. Is that right?
YesNo
Yes
Received. Thank you for letting us know.

The rules are checked automatically: the end date cannot be before the start, the days cannot be in the past, the period is at most 90 days and it cannot overlap another active period. See Panelist availability for what happens next.

Where to find things

In the ONE menu, open the Incidents group:

Reported incidents

Everything panelists report through Telegram (missing materials, damaged or returned parcels, contact changes…).

The menu shows a red badge for items that wait for you, and a blue badge when a panelist has written a reply you have not read yet.

Examples

Names and figures are illustrative.

1. Damaged parcel — closes by itself

  1. Marta writes “The package arrived broken” and taps Yes.
  2. The incident appears as Auto-resolved, with the whole chat inside it.
  3. You do nothing.

2. Returned parcel — you decide

  1. Pablo writes “They sent my package back” and confirms. The incident is Open and you get an email.
  2. A returned package goes back to the sender, who keeps the label.
  3. Choose one:
    • Cancel shipment, if the sample is no longer needed.
    • Re-send returned sample: pick a Destination node in the same city and a Week, check the preview of the load and press Confirm re-send.
  4. Write a message to Pablo and press Resolve.

3. Contact data change — closes by itself

  1. Karim writes “My new phone is 600 123 456”.
  2. The bot asks which field to change, validates the value and asks for a Yes / No.
  3. The phone number is updated and the incident records the old and new value.

Missing materials

This is the only type with several automated steps, because material often has to travel between panelists.

  1. The panelist reports it. “I’m out of envelopes and seals” → confirmed with Yes.
  2. You open the incident and see three things: the stock ONE believes the panelist has, the need for the period, and the proposed supply. ONE covers the need in this order: neighbouring panelists in the same city with surplus, then the central warehouse, then purchase.
  3. You press Confirm supply (you can change the origin of any material first). ONE creates the Material shipments sheet, which you can view, Print / PDF or download as Excel (CSV).
  4. Telegram does the rest.
    • The donor receives the materials, quantities and the receiver’s name and address only, with an I have sent it button.
    • When the date arrives, the receiver is asked whether the material arrived and how much: All or Other quantity.
    • If only part arrived, ONE records it and asks again later about the rest.
  5. The panelist is not pushed to do the impossible. Shipments that need the missing material are left out of the daily reminder. When the material arrives, the bot tells them: You can now send these…
  6. The incident closes by itself when everything has been received.
No reply from a donor or a receiver? After two unanswered questions to a donor, or three to a receiver, you get one email. If a receiver has no Telegram, ONE skips the question and emails you once.

Panelist availability

An unavailable period is a time when a panelist cannot send or receive samples (holidays, illness, lack of material…). Every period becomes an incident, and ONE tries to fix the samples affected without you.

How a period is declared

  • Telegram buttons: Unavailable days in the bot menu.
  • Telegram free text: “I’m on holiday next week”. The bot reads the dates and asks to confirm. You also receive an email.
  • ONE: from the Panelists screen.

In every case the panelist sees Received. Thank you for letting us know.

What ONE does with each affected sample

For every sample scheduled during the period where the panelist sends or receives, ONE tries these in order:

  1. Move to a later date. The first working day (Monday to Friday) after the period ends, within the same month, when the node is available. Samples are spread across days.
  2. Move to another node in the same city. The node with the lowest load that would not become saturated; first in the same week, then other dates in the month.
  3. Leave it to you. If neither works, the sample waits for your decision with a proposal already calculated.
Rules that are never broken. A sample already sent or received is never touched. A sample never moves to another city. A node is never overloaded just to resolve quickly. Shipments only fall on working days.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
NewJust declared; ONE is calculating.
Resolved automaticallyAll samples were moved, or none were affected.
Pending manager decisionAt least one sample needs you.
ResolvedYou decided all pending samples.

If the plan changes after the period was declared, ONE recalculates it. Panelists affected by a change in the current week receive the details of the new shipment on Telegram.

Worked example

Figures are illustrative.

ONE bot
From 10 to 21 November I’ll be on holiday
I understood 10 Nov – 21 Nov. Is that right?
YesNo
Yes
Received. Thank you for letting us know.

Elena had 14 samples scheduled in that period. ONE summarises its work in the incident:

9 moved to a later date · 3 sent to another node · 2 pending
  • 9 moved: to the first free working days after 21 November, within November.
  • 3 to another node: Elena had no possible date in the month, so they went to other nodes in the city with low load. Those panelists are told on Telegram.
  • 2 pending: no node could take them without becoming saturated. The incident is Pending manager decision.

What you do

  1. Open Panelist availability and select Elena’s incident. You see all her samples (resolved and pending) and the city’s load by week.
  2. Each pending sample has a proposal. Select the lines and press Accept selected, or press Confirm on a single line.
  3. To do something different, use Change resolution and choose Reroute to another node, Change date or Cancel sample, then Apply. Cancel selected or Void sample remove samples you do not need.
  4. When no line is pending, the incident becomes Resolved.
Unavailability because of missing material. From a missing-materials incident you can create an unavailable period that runs from today until the material is expected. It follows exactly the same process.

Conversation and alerts

Each incident has a Conversation with the panelist that keeps the bot’s messages, the panelist’s replies (with photos) and yours.

  • Writing to the panelist. Type in the incident and press Send. It reaches their Telegram, usually within five minutes, as plain text.
  • Unread replies. Free-text replies from the panelist show a blue badge in the menu and in the list until you open the incident. Buttons the panelist taps do not count.
  • Email alert. If a reply stays unread for more than an hour, you get one email per incident. If you open it first, no email is sent.
  • Closing. Resolving or dismissing an incident closes the Telegram conversation.

Questions

What if a panelist types something random?
The bot shows the menu or suggests a type. Nothing is saved until the panelist presses Yes.
Does ONE ever move a sample that was already sent?
No. Only pending samples are moved. A returned package is re-sent from the incident, by your decision.
Can samples go to another city?
No. Changing city would change the measured route.
Why is a panelist no longer reminded of some shipments?
Those shipments need material the panelist does not have. ONE stops insisting until the material arrives, then tells them they can send.
Do shipments fall on weekends?
No. Shipments are scheduled Monday to Friday only.
A panelist says they reported something but I cannot see it.
Check that they confirmed with Yes. If they answered No, or did not answer, nothing was saved.
Who can see an incident?
Each account sees only its own panelists and incidents.
