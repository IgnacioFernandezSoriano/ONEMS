Panelist guide: the ONE bot on Telegram

With the ONE bot, everything is done with buttons. Here is what each button does, what to choose if you have a problem and what can happen next.

Three rules

👆

Tap the buttons

The bot always offers you the options. You don't need to type commands.

⚠️

A problem: “Report incident”

Tap that button and pick what happened from the list.

✍️

Type only when asked

A number, a code or a new detail. And to talk to your manager.

Open the bot

Open the ONE bot chat in Telegram and type /start. The menu appears.

Lost? Type /start at any time and you are back at the menu.

The bot talks to you in the language of your profile. To change it, see Change my details.

The 09:00 reminder

Every morning at 09:00 the bot sends you the list of parcels you have to send that day. You don't need to ask for it. Parcels are sent Monday to Friday only.

Send a parcel

  1. Tap Confirm a shipment.
  2. Type the number of the parcel in the list.
  3. Take a photo of the label and send it, or type the label code.

What happens with each problem

📦 I'm missing materials

You have run out of envelopes, seals or other materials to prepare your parcels.

  1. Tap Report incident and then 📦 I'm missing materials.
  2. Done. Your manager already knows and arranges the delivery.
What can happen next:
  • You get materials from another panelist in your city or from the head office. When they should have arrived, the bot asks you “Did you receive the materials…?” (see).
  • Until they arrive, the bot stops reminding you about the parcels you can't send. This is not an error.
  • If it will take a while, your parcels for those days may go to other panelists. The bot tells you.
  • When you receive the materials, the bot tells you “You can send these now: …” with the date and destination of each one (see).
  • Your manager may write to you to ask something (see).

💥 Broken or damaged parcel

When it arrives, the box is broken, wet or open.

  1. Tap Report incident and then 💥 Broken or damaged parcel.
  2. If you can read the label, confirm the reception as usual.
What can happen next: nothing else. It is recorded for the measurement and you don't have to do anything.

↩️ A parcel was returned to me

A parcel you sent has come back to your home.

  1. Open the parcel in View pending shipments if it is there, and tap ⚠️ Report incident. If not, use Report incident.
  2. Choose ↩️ A parcel was returned to me.
  3. Keep the parcel and its label. Don't throw them away.
What can happen next:
  • Your manager may ask you to send it again to another destination in the same city. It will show up in your pending shipments with its new date.
  • Or they may cancel it: then you don't have to do anything with it.
  • If they need something (for example, a photo of the label), they write to you in this chat.

🔍 I can't read a label

A parcel has arrived and its label is faded, torn or covered.

  1. Tap Report incident and then 🔍 I can't read a label.
  2. The bot shows you the parcels on their way to you. Tap ✅ This one on the one that arrived.
  3. If it isn't there, tap ❌ None.
What can happen next:
  • If you pick one: it is marked as received. No photo needed.
  • If it isn't in the list: “I can't find any parcels on their way to you; a manager will look into it.” Your manager will write to you if they need anything.

📷 I can't take the tag photo

You are confirming a shipment and the camera doesn't work or the photo comes out wrong.

  1. Tap Report incident and then 📷 I can't take the tag photo.
  2. If the bot asks, choose the parcel from your list.
  3. Type the tag code, letter by letter.
What can happen next: the shipment is confirmed with the code, just like with the photo. Your manager doesn't have to do anything.
Tip: take the photo in good light, up close and without glare. That way you won't have to type the code.

📱 Change my details

You have a new phone number, email or address, or you want the bot in another language.

  1. Tap Report incident and then 📱 Change my details.
  2. Choose which detail you want to change.
  3. Type the new detail (the language is chosen with buttons) and confirm it.
What can happen next: “Details updated.” The change applies right away. If the detail is not valid, the bot tells you and nothing changes.

🗓️ Unavailable days

Holiday, illness, training or personal reasons: there are days when you can't send or receive.

  1. Tap 🗓️ Unavailable days (in the menu or in the list of problems) and then ➕ Declare unavailable days.
  2. Tap Today or Tomorrow. For other days, tap ✏️ From–to and type the dates like this: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-13 (or a single date for one day).
  3. Check the dates and tap ✅ Confirm.

With 📋 View my unavailable days you can see the ones you have and cancel them with ❌ Cancel these days.

What can happen next:
  • The parcels you had on those days move to another date or to another panelist in your city. You don't have to do anything.
  • On those days the bot doesn't send you the 09:00 reminder.
  • Your manager may write to you if they need anything.
Let us know in advance. Future dates only, and at most 90 days in a row.

Messages you may get

Sometimes the bot writes to you first. These are the messages you may receive and what to tap.

📬 “Did you receive the materials…?”

Materials were sent to you (because you were missing some) and the bot wants to know if you have them.

  1. If you have them, tap Yes. If not, tap Not yet: it will ask you again later.
  2. For each material, tap All if it all arrived, or Another amount and type the number that arrived.
What can happen next:
  • If everything arrived, it is closed and the bot tells you which parcels you can send now.
  • If something is missing, it will ask you about the rest later.
  • If you don't answer after several questions, your manager gets a notice and may write to you.
Did it arrive and the bot hasn't asked you? Tap 📦 Confirm material received in the menu.

🤝 Send materials to another panelist

You have spare materials and another panelist in your city is missing some. This can happen even if you haven't reported anything.

  1. The bot tells you which material, how much, to whom (name and address) and by when it must arrive.
  2. Prepare it and send it by regular mail.
  3. When you have sent it, tap I've sent it. Once is enough.
What can happen next:
  • That material is subtracted from your stock. You don't have to do anything else.
  • If you don't tap the button, the bot reminds you every two days. After two reminders, it notifies your manager.
  • If you can't send it, tap Report incident → 💬 Another problem and your manager will write to you.
Privacy: you will only see the other panelist's name and address, never their phone or email.

📌 Message from your manager

Your manager writes to you about something you reported. It is the only time you type freely.

  1. Read the message. The top line says what it is about.
  2. Tap ✍️ Reply and type whatever you need. You can also send photos.
  3. When you finish, tap ↩️ Back to menu.
What can happen next: your manager reads your reply and may write to you again. The conversation closes by itself when the matter is resolved or after 48 hours without messages.

Questions

Do I have to type to report a problem?
No. Tap Report incident and choose from the list. You only type if your manager asks you something.
I tapped the wrong button.
Tap ↩️ Back to menu if it appears. If it was already registered, choose 💬 Another problem and explain it to your manager when they write to you.
Why is it no longer reminding me about some parcels?
Because you are missing the materials to send them. When they arrive, the bot will tell you “You can send these now”.
Can parcels be taken away from me?
Yes, if you report unavailable days or if you are missing materials for a while: they move to another date or to another panelist in your city.
Can I be asked to send materials to another panelist?
Yes, if you have spare ones. The bot tells you what, how much, to whom and by when. Tap “I've sent it” when you send them.
Are there parcels at the weekend?
No. Parcels are sent Monday to Friday only.

The bot's messages may change slightly depending on your language and your case. The names, dates and codes in this guide are examples.
