Case analysis

How to ask the ONEMS operations agent to analyze a case with a panelist, from the ONEMS screen or from the Telegram analysis bot, and what to do with the result.

What it is

When a panelist has a problem (a receipt that does not match, a reported incident, a leave), the ONEMS operations agent gathers the data from the plan and from what happened and gives you a short answer: what is happening, why and what to do. It also prepares a message for each panelist involved.

Always on request

Nothing is ever analyzed on its own. The analysis starts when you press Analyze case in ONEMS or 🔎 Analyse on Telegram.

A short card

The result is a card with Problem, Cause and Solution. The rest is under Show detail.

Nothing is sent on its own

Messages to panelists are drafts. None goes out until you press Send.

One limit per account

Each analysis costs a few cents. Each account has a limit of $5 per month, shared between ONEMS and the bot.

Available in all active accounts, including new ones. What is controlled per account is who can sign up to the bot and the monthly limit.

Analyzing a case in ONEMS

The button is in the detail of the three types of case that appear in the Incidents group of the menu:

  • Receipts: in the case row, press Analyze; a Case 00012345 window opens with the Analyze case button.
  • Incidents reported by panelists (Reported incidents): open the incident; the Analyze case button is in its detail.
  • Panelist availability: open the leave or unavailability; the Analyze case button is in its detail.

If something fails. The analysis failed. Try again. or The agent is not responding. Try again later. Press the button again. Signing up to the bot

You only need to do this once. Your ONEMS email must appear in your account's record with one of the authorized roles.

  1. On Telegram, search for @PanelistEventsAnalysisBot, open the chat and press Start (or type /start).
  2. The bot asks for your ONEMS email. Type it.
  3. The bot answers the same thing every time, whether or not the email is valid. If it is, you receive a 6-digit code by email.
  4. Type the code in the chat.
  5. The bot confirms the account you are in: «You can now analyse cases of …».

Opening a case on Telegram

A case is everything you send before pressing 🔎 Analyse. Opening a case and adding material to it costs nothing: only Analyse and Change use the analysis.

What you can send

  • Forwarded messages from the conversation with the panelist.
  • Screenshots or photos, including those sent as a file: up to 5 MB each and 6 per case. A larger image is not used; those beyond the sixth are ignored.
  • Text written by you telling what happened. If the text has at least two words or a number (for example, a tag), it opens a case.
  1. Forward the messages, send the screenshots or write what happened.
  2. The bot replies Received (1)… and shows the 🔎 Analyse and ✖️ Discard buttons. You can keep adding material.
  3. Press 🔎 Analyse.
  4. The bot replies Analysing… and, when it finishes, sends the result in a new message.

Fix R4: redirect and register the receipt

It rests on one rule: a tag that is correctly identified at origin and at destination is never lost. If a panelist registered a tag that was not for them (that of a shipment to another city) and the plan line is identified beyond doubt, the bot offers the ✅ Apply button on the card.

If R4 is not possible in that case, the button does not appear. It is only applied from Telegram; not in ONEMS.

  1. Press ✅ Apply. The bot shows the preview: tag, plan line, current and new destination, origin, receipt date and SLA deadline.
  2. Review it. If it is not what you want, press Do not apply (Not applied.).
  3. If it is correct, press Confirm. The line moves to the real addressee, is registered as received and the receipt case becomes Fixed.
  4. For 24 hours ↩️ Undo appears.

Who has access

Access to the bot is managed in ONEMS; there is nothing to configure in the bot. People whose email is in their account's record with one of these roles can sign up:

Account record fieldWhat it is generally for
Panelist Manager emailManages the panelists: receives incident, leave and bot alerts.
Account Manager emailProject lead for this account. Receives the operations reports.
Allocation Plan Manager emailPerson in charge of the allocation plan of this account; uses the case analysis bot.
  1. An ONEMS superadmin opens the Administration › Accounts menu (Account Management screen) and edits the account. If you are not a superadmin, ask your ONEMS contact.
  2. Under Account contacts, type or delete the person's email in the field of the corresponding role.
  3. Save. The person can now sign up to the bot; if you delete their email, they lose access within a few minutes at most.
  • One person, one country. The same email cannot appear in several accounts: it is not signed up and a notice is sent by email.
  • ONEMS superadmins. They can work with any country: they choose it with buttons when signing up and change it by typing country.
  • Test accounts are not accepted.
  • The account sees and analyzes only its own panelists.
And then: you do not need to notify the bot. Each use checks the email in ONEMS again (with a 5-minute memory).

The drafts always follow the same rules to be brief and clear. Read them and change them if needed before sending.

AddresseeNot written to, unless they have to do something.

  • If the analysis does not lead to any case, the bot sends it as a notice: Sent to … as a notice.

Questions

Does it spend anything if I only forward messages?
No. Opening a case and adding messages, screenshots or text to it is free. Only 🔎 Analyse, choosing or typing the panelist when the bot asks, and ✏️ Change spend.
Can I analyze cases from another country?
No. You only see the panelists of your account. If you type one from another country, the bot replies I cannot find that panelist in your account. ONEMS superadmins can choose a country.
Is anything sent to panelists without my knowledge?
No. The analysis is always on request and messages only go out when you press Send.
What happens if I make a mistake when applying fix R4?
Press ↩️ Undo within the next 24 hours: the plan line and the receipt case are back as they were. After 24 hours, it can no longer be undone from the bot.
Why does the bot speak to me in Spanish (or French)?
Because it speaks your account's language. The drafts are also in the language of each panelist.
What if the code does not arrive?
Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. Check with whoever administers your account that your email is in the record with one of the authorized roles. If you type it and it is not there, the bot answers the same and nothing arrives. Remember the limit of 3 codes per hour; a code is valid for 15 minutes and allows 5 attempts.
It says «Your access is no longer valid»
Your email was removed from the account or no longer has an authorized role. If it was a mistake, ask for it to be restored in ONEMS and sign up again by typing your email.
It says «Case analysis is not enabled for your account yet»
Analysis is available for all active accounts. If you see this, notify the ONEMS team.
Can I use it in a Telegram group?
No. It only works in the private chat with the bot.
Where does the analysis end up?
As an internal note on the case (on Telegram, with your email as the requester). Sent messages stay in the case conversation.
