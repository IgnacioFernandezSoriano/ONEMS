Roles and access

What each person sees and can do in ONEMS depends on their roles in their country’s account. This page explains the roles, the rights and how the Account Manager and the UPU account manager manage them.

Video: roles and access in 3 minutes

The six roles

A role is a person’s job in their country’s account. Each role gives some rights: which menu options the person sees and where they can change data. There are always these six roles, in every account.

RoleWhat it does
Account ManagerHead of the account. Manages almost everything and decides the access of the team.
Allocation Plan ManagerPrepares and adjusts the shipment plans: generator, load balancing and allocation plans.
Panelist ManagerManages the panelists, their incidents and their availability.
Materials SupplierManages materials: requirements, stock and catalog.
DO coordinatorCoordinates the RFID side: postal centers, readers and segment SLA.
UPU account managerUPU person who follows the account. Has wide rights and also decides access.
Role names are shown in English in every language, as in ONEMS.

Edit, View and no access

For each menu option, a role has one of these three rights:

Edit

Sees the screen and can change data: create, save, delete or import.

View

Sees the screen but cannot change anything. The create, save and delete buttons are hidden and the screen shows the View only badge.

—

The option does not appear in the menu.

Several roles add up

A person can have several roles. For each option the highest right wins: Edit over View, and View over nothing.

Example: in the template, the Panelist Manager has View on Stock Management and the Materials Supplier has Edit. Someone with both roles has Edit.

Users with no role

An account user with no role only sees reporting and the Help center, in View:

  • Territory Equity
  • Compliance
  • Route Analysis
  • Segment Analysis
  • Help center

They do not see the Dashboard. When they log in, ONEMS opens the first of these screens.

This list is the same in every account and cannot be changed. If someone needs more, give them a role.

Roles in several accounts

A person can have roles in several countries; for example, a UPU account manager who follows several accounts. In that case the Account selector appears at the top of the menu.

  1. Choose the account in the Account selector.
  2. The page reloads by itself.
  3. You now see the menus, rights and data of that account.
You work with one account at a time. If ONEMS is open in two browser tabs, both share the same account: after switching in one, reload the other.

Asking for an access change

If a menu option is missing, or you need Edit where you only have View, ask the Account Manager or the UPU account manager of your account. Only they can change the roles and rights of the account.

Then: the change is saved at once. Reload the page to see your updated menu.

The Roles and access tab

This part is for the Account Manager and the UPU account manager: they are the only people in the account who can change its access. This permission is fixed: it cannot be removed from the rights matrix.

Where it is

  • In the menu Administration › Roles and access. Only the Account Manager and the UPU account manager see it. It opens the tab of the account you are working in.
  • The UPU superadmin also has it in each account’s form, in the Roles and access tab, next to General.

Account contacts

There are six emails, one per role. ONEMS sends the account’s automatic notices and emails to them, such as the operations report.

A contact is not the same as a role. Putting an email in the contacts does not give access to ONEMS. And having a role does not mean receiving the notices: only the email written in the contacts receives them.
  1. Type the email of each role. Leave it empty if that role does not exist in your account.
  2. Press Save contacts.
Then: Saved appears and the next notices go to the new emails. The change is listed in Latest changes.

Operations report

The “ONE operations report” email goes to the account contacts; the Account Manager and the UPU account manager are in copy. Each account has its own schedule: some days of the week, one day a month, or never. The UPU superadmin sets it in the account’s General tab. To change it, ask your UPU account manager.

Changing a right

The Rights of each role table has one row per menu option, grouped as in the menu, and one column per role.

  1. Find the row of the menu option.
  2. In the role’s column, open the selector and choose Edit, View or —.
  3. There is no save button: the change is saved at once.
Then: everyone with that role in this account has the new right. They see it when they reload the page. Other accounts do not change.
The No role column is fixed: it is the same in every account and cannot be changed.

User roles

The Users and their roles table lists the users of the account, with one checkbox per role. A user can have several roles.

  1. Find the user.
  2. Tick the role’s checkbox to give it, or untick it to remove it.
  3. The change is saved at once.

“Provisional” roles

When roles were switched on, ONEMS assigned some automatically. They carry the Provisional mark and an orange checkbox. Review them:

  • If the role is right, press Confirm next to the mark. The mark disappears. You can also confirm your own role.
  • If it is wrong, untick it and tick the right role.
You cannot remove your own last management role. If it is your only Account Manager or UPU account manager role in the account, ONEMS will not let you remove it, so that nobody loses the ability to fix this tab.

Add an existing user by email is for the superadmin only. It gives a role in your account to someone who is already a user of another account. Ask UPU.

Then: the user has the new rights when they reload the page. The change is listed in Latest changes.

Back to template

Each account starts from the rights template and can adjust it. The Back to template button undoes all changes to your account’s matrix.

  1. Press Back to template.
  2. ONEMS asks for confirmation. Accept.
Then: all rights of the account go back to the template. User roles and contacts do not change.

Latest changes

The Latest changes list shows the changes made in the tab: Date, Who made it and the Change; for example, Panelist Manager · Panelists: View → Edit. Automatic changes appear as System.

Creating users with roles

People with Edit on Users can create users. In the template, the Account Manager and the UPU account manager.

  1. Open Administration › Users.
  2. Press Create User.
  3. Fill in the user’s details: Email, Password, Full Name…
  4. In Roles in the account (optional), tick their roles.
  5. Press Create.
Then: the user appears in Users and their roles with those roles. With no role ticked, they only see reporting (see Users with no role).
Only the Account Manager and the UPU account manager see the role checkboxes. If someone else creates the user, give the roles afterwards in the tab.

Questions

A screen shows “View only”. Why?
Your roles only give you View on that option. If you need to change data, ask the Account Manager or the UPU account manager of your account for Edit.
A menu option is missing.
None of your roles has access to it. Ask the Account Manager or the UPU account manager. If you have several accounts, check in the Account selector that you are in the right one.
I have the role but I do not get the automatic notices.
Notices go to the emails in Account contacts, not to users with the role. Ask for your email to be added to the contacts.
Does a change in my account affect other countries?
No. Each account has its own rights, contacts and roles.
Can I create a new role or rename one?
No. The six roles are the same in every account. What each account adjusts is its rights.
