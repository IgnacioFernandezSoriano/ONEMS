GPS location when declaring

The ONE bot records the position of the panelist's phone when they declare a shipment or a receipt. This lets you check that they do it at the post office or at their PO box, and see who is not providing it.

What it is and why

Only when declaring

The bot asks for the location when the panelist starts confirming a shipment or a receipt. There is never continuous tracking.

Compared with the office

ONEMS measures the distance to the nearest postal center in the panelist's city that has coordinates.

You see it in two places

In Incidents › Reported incidents (category GPS location) and in the operations report email.

It is evidence, not absolute proof. A location can be faked with special apps, and a position given by the mobile network (without GPS) can be hundreds of metres off. Use it to spot patterns and talk to the panelist, not to sanction a single case.

The golden rule

A declaration is never blocked or lost because of GPS. If the panelist gives no permission, does not share the location or their GPS fails, the shipment or receipt is registered anyway. Only the location status is recorded and, if needed, an incident is opened for you.

The permission

The first time the bot is about to ask for the location, it first asks the panelist whether they allow it, with ✅ I accept / ❌ I don't accept. They are asked only once.

  • The exact text they saw, in their language, when and what they answered is recorded. The current text (version 2) says the location is seen by their account's managers and UPU's technical team.
  • If they do not answer and declare anyway, they are asked again on the next visit.
  • They can change their decision at any time in 📖 Help → 📍 Location permission. Every change is recorded.
  • If they do not accept, the bot never asks them for the location again and you receive one incident so you can talk to them.

"GPS location" incident

Every GPS problem opens an incident with category GPS location in Incidents › Reported incidents.

  • One incident per visit, not per parcel: it groups all the shipments and receipts of that visit, with the tag, the kind (Sent / Received), the status and the Distance to reference.
  • It opens when a declaration is left without a position, with "GPS doesn't work", with an imprecise position or far from the office.
  • It becomes Auto-resolved if a valid position arrives within 30 minutes and all its declarations become OK.
  • If the panelist presses ❌ I don't accept, a single incident is opened (not one per visit) with the text The panelist did not give permission to record their location.
Then: talk to the panelist if needed and resolve the incident with a note, like any other. The shipment or receipt is already registered: there is nothing to fix in it.

Operations report

The Monday, Wednesday and Friday report email shows GPS in three places.

"GPS" column (panelists annex)

It shows ok/total in the period and, if any, how many are far or whether there is no permission. For example, 7/10 · 2 far.

  • Orange if less than 80% of their declarations have an OK position, or if any is far.
  • Red if they gave no permission, or if they have 3 or more declarations and none OK.

Section "7. Who is not registering with GPS"

It lists the panelists with their city, the main reason and how many declarations are affected. The reasons are: did not give permission, says their GPS does not work, does not send the location, has not answered the permission request and imprecise position.

Panelists who do send a position but far away are not in this section: you will see them in the GPS column and in the Unusual registration column ("N with position far from the office").

Configuration review

  • GPS reference: cities without an office with coordinates: their panelists' position cannot be checked.
  • Doubtful GPS reference: in that city, the median distance from positions to the office is over 500 m. Most likely the postal center coordinate is wrong, not the panelist. Fix it in Postal Centers.

What to do

  1. Check the Who is not registering with GPS section of the report and call those panelists.
  2. Ask them to declare from the phone, with location turned on in Settings, and to press 📍 Share my location. Remind them that the panelist guide has a Location (GPS) section.
  3. If a panelist gave no permission, explain what it is for and that they can change it in 📖 Help → 📍 Location permission. The decision is theirs.
  4. If Doubtful GPS reference or cities without an office with coordinates appears, check the coordinates of that city's postal centers in Postal Centers.
  5. If a panelist is repeatedly far and the reference is good, talk to them before drawing conclusions.
Then: the next report shows the change. The reference review only uses positions from the report period, so a corrected coordinate stops appearing in the next one.

Privacy

  • The position is asked only when declaring. There is never continuous tracking or live location.
  • It is visible to the users of the panelist's account and to the UPU superadmin. No other account.
  • Exact coordinates are deleted after 12 months (daily task). The distance and the status are kept.
  • The record of each permission (text shown, language, date and answer) is always kept, even if the panelist is removed.
  • The bot interaction log keeps no coordinates and is deleted after 60 days.

Questions

Can the panelist be forced to give the location?
No. The location never blocks a declaration and the permission is the panelist's decision. You see who does not give it and can talk to them.
A panelist shows as "far" but is at the office. Why?
Most often the coordinate of the reference postal center is wrong, or their office is not the registered postal center. Check Postal Centers. It can also be an inaccurate network position.
Why is there an incident that resolved itself?
The panelist declared before sharing the location and shared it within 30 minutes. The position was applied to the whole visit.
Can the panelist declare from a computer?
Yes, but Telegram on a computer does not send the location: those declarations will have no position.
Is there a map with the positions in ONEMS?
Not for now. The information is in the incident and in the operations report.
