Incidents and panelist availability

How panelists report problems and unavailable days through Telegram, what ONEMS resolves on its own, and what you decide as a manager.

Overview

Panelists use Telegram

They chat with the ONE bot in their own language (English, Spanish, French or Arabic) to confirm shipments, report a problem or declare unavailable days.

ONEMS resolves what it can

Many reports are closed automatically. Unavailable days are re-planned by moving samples to a later date or to another node in the same city.

Managers decide the rest

What cannot be resolved automatically appears in Incidents with a proposal ready, and every message is saved in the incident.

This page has two audiences. Panelist and bot sections describe what the panelist sees. Manager sections describe what you see in ONEMS.

  • Nothing is saved until the panelist answers Yes. If they answer No, the bot shows the menu again.
  • Everything the bot says, everything the panelist answers and everything you write is stored in the incident.
  • You get an email when a new incident needs you, and when a panelist reply has been waiting for more than an hour.

Starting the bot

Each panelist is linked to the ONE bot by their Telegram account. Open the chat and send /start, or just say hello. The bot greets them by first name and shows the main menu.

  • The bot answers in the language set in the panelist’s profile.
  • Typing something that is not a request (“Hello”, “thanks”) just shows the menu again.
  • /myid shows the panelist’s Telegram ID. Support may ask for it when linking an account.

Sending and receiving

Confirming a shipment

  1. The panelist taps Confirm a shipment or View pending shipments and picks the shipment.
  2. They send a photo of the label or type the label number.
  3. The bot replies that the shipment is declared as sent. If the label format is not valid, nothing is saved and the bot asks again.

Confirming a reception

  1. The panelist taps Confirm a reception.
  2. They send a photo of the label of the package received.
  3. ONEMS marks that sample as received. If the label is unreadable, they report it as an incident instead (Unreadable label / receipt), choose the package from their list and it is registered automatically.
Daily reminder. Every day at 09:00 the bot sends each panelist the shipments to send that day. Shipments that cannot be made because of missing material are left out of the reminder.

Reporting an incident

The panelist taps Report incident and describes the problem in a single message. The bot suggests a type and asks for confirmation.

Do you want to register this as a damaged parcel?
YesNo, it’s something else
Yes
Registered, thanks for letting us know.

A damaged parcel is saved and closed automatically.

ONE bot
They sent my package back
Do you want to register this as a returned parcel?
YesNo, it’s something else
Yes
Registered. A manager will contact you.

A returned parcel stays open for the manager.

Safe by design. If the panelist taps No, or types something unclear, nothing is registered. Only an explicit Yes creates an incident.

Unavailable days

The panelist taps 🗓️ Unavailable days and chooses the start and end dates. They can also write it in plain words.

ONE bot
Next week I’m on holiday
I understood Mon 9 Nov – Fri 13 Nov. Is that right?
YesNo
Yes
Received. Thank you for letting us know.

The rules are checked automatically: the end date cannot be before the start, the days cannot be in the past, the period is at most 90 days and it cannot overlap another active period. See Panelist availability for what happens next.

Handling each type in ONEMS

What the action block shows for each type, and what to do. Names and figures are illustrative.

Returned parcel — you decide

A returned package goes back to the sender, who keeps the label. The action block is Re-send returned sample (shown in the detail above).

  1. Pick a Destination node. Only nodes of the same city as the current destination are offered; if there is none, the screen says No other node in this city.
  2. Pick a Week, from the current one onwards. ONEMS checks the day and the load and shows Fits: date (load n), or the reason why it cannot be moved there.
  3. Press Confirm re-send. The sample keeps its tag and goes back into the plan as pending. The panelists concerned are told on Telegram.
  4. Or press Cancel shipment if the sample is no longer needed. This cannot be undone from this screen.
  5. Write to the panelist if useful, add a resolution note and press Resolve.

Automatic types — read only

For Contact data change and Unreadable label / receipt the action block is titled Handled automatically and only shows what happened.

What happens next on Telegram

  1. The donor receives the materials, quantities and the receiver’s name and address only, with an I have sent it button.
  2. When the date arrives, the receiver is asked whether the material arrived and how much: All or Other quantity. If only part arrived, ONEMS records it and asks again later about the rest.
  3. Shipments that need the missing material are left out of the receiver’s daily reminder. When the material arrives, the bot tells them: You can now send these…
  4. The incident closes by itself when everything has been received. The leave, if any, is not cancelled: it ends on its own date.
No reply from a donor or a receiver? After two unanswered questions to a donor, or three to a receiver, you get one email. If a receiver has no Telegram, ONEMS skips the question and emails you once.

Panelist availability

An unavailable period is a time when a panelist cannot send or receive samples (holidays, illness, lack of material…). Every period becomes an incident, and ONEMS tries to fix the samples affected without you.

How a period is declared

  • Telegram buttons: Unavailable days in the bot menu.
  • Telegram free text: “I’m on holiday next week”. The bot reads the dates and asks to confirm. You also receive an email.
  • ONEMS: from the Panelists screen (next section).

In every case the panelist sees Received. Thank you for letting us know.

What ONEMS does with each affected sample

For every sample scheduled during the period where the panelist sends or receives, ONEMS tries these in order:

  1. Move to a later date. The first working day (Monday to Friday) after the period ends, within the same month, when the node is available. Samples are spread across days.
  2. Move to another node in the same city. The node with the lowest load that would not become saturated; first in the same week, then other dates in the month.
  3. Leave it to you. If neither works, the sample waits for your decision with a proposal already calculated.
Rules that are never broken. A sample already sent or received is never touched. A sample never moves to another city. A node is never overloaded just to resolve quickly. Shipments only fall on working days.

If the plan changes after the period was declared, ONEMS recalculates it. Panelists affected by a change in the current week receive the details of the new shipment on Telegram.

  3. Pending your decision. One line per sample the engine could not resolve, with its role, original date, the System proposal and its cause. Confirm applies the proposal to that line; Void sample cancels the sample.
  4. Accept selected / Cancel selected do the same for several lines at once. Cancelling a sample removes it from the allocation plan and cannot be undone from this screen, so ONEMS asks you to confirm.
  5. Change resolution. To do something other than the proposal, choose a Final action: Reroute to another node (pick the Target node), Change date (pick the New date) or Cancel sample, then Apply.

The panelists are informed. Every change you apply to a sample in the current week is sent on Telegram to the panelists concerned, with the new date or node.

9 moved to a later date · 3 moved to another node · 2 pending
  • 9 moved: to the first free working days after 21 November, within November.
  • 3 to another node: Elena had no possible date in the month, so they went to other nodes in the city with low load. Those panelists are told on Telegram.
  • 2 pending: no node could take them without becoming saturated. The incident is Pending manager decision.

What you do

  1. Open Panelist availability and press Review on Elena’s incident. You see the summary, the city balance and her samples, resolved and pending (the screen above).
  2. The first pending sample proposes NY-02 in week 48, and the balance shows NY-02 is Normal that week: press Confirm.
  3. The second proposes cancelling. You would rather keep it: press Change resolution, choose Change date, pick the first working day of December and Apply.
  4. Write a Final resolution note and Save. The incident becomes Resolved; Elena and the other panelists concerned are told on Telegram.
Unavailability because of missing material. From a missing-materials incident you can create an unavailable period that runs from today until the material is expected. It follows exactly the same process; the reason shows as Materials.

Questions

What if a panelist types something random?
The bot shows the menu or suggests a type. Nothing is saved until the panelist presses Yes.
Does ONEMS ever move a sample that was already sent?
No. Only pending samples are moved. A returned package is re-sent from the incident, by your decision.
Can samples go to another city?
No. Changing city would change the measured route.
Why is a panelist no longer reminded of some shipments?
Those shipments need material the panelist does not have. ONEMS stops insisting until the material arrives, then tells them they can send.
Do shipments fall on weekends?
No. Shipments are scheduled Monday to Friday only.
A panelist says they reported something but I cannot see it.
Check that they confirmed with Yes. If they answered No, or did not answer, nothing was saved.
Who can see an incident?
Each account sees only its own panelists and incidents.
